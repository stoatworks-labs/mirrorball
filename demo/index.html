Photosensitivity warning

On their defaults neither plugin flashes, and with the default 50 cm ball and the lamp at 5 m nothing went over the line at any setting tried. They can flash when the spots are few, big and fast: a small ball (20 cm) with the pin spot in close (about a metre or nearer), turning fast. Flashing light can trigger seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy.

Broadcast and web guidance (Ofcom's, and WCAG's) puts the line at three flashes in any second, six rises and falls in brightness. Measured from the plugin's own output, the worst third of the picture went over it with that small ball and close lamp at 5 rpm in Free (10 rises and falls at +4 stops), in Sync with no Glide above six steps a second (8 for 90° every half beat at 240 BPM), and in Sync with any Glide, which turns the ball far faster than Free's 5 rpm (16 for 90° every half beat at 120 BPM).

The page opens on the defaults, and no preset is in that region. A link that opens with the ball 0.3 m or smaller, Throw 1.5 m or nearer and the ball turning fast (over 3 rpm in Free; in Sync, any Glide or more than six steps a second) opens paused. Pause, under the picture, stops it at once.
